NUFI is an **AI platform** for teams and organisations. Think
Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — but with the AI models you choose, the
budget controls you decide, and a developer console for building on
top.

If you have ever wished ChatGPT could use your company's preferred
model, respect your team budgets, and let your developers call it
from their own code with self-served API keys — that is NUFI.

## What you can do with NUFI

- **Chat with any AI model.** NUFI works with frontier models from
  OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, Meta, and others — plus
  open-source models you host yourself. Switch between them per
  conversation.
- **Work with files and images.** Drop PDFs, docs, code, screenshots
  into a conversation. The AI reads them and answers in context.
- **Build agents.** No-code AI assistants with custom instructions,
  tools, and knowledge files — shareable with your team.
- **Generate images.** From text prompts, or by editing existing
  images.
- **Search the web.** When the AI needs live information, it can
  search and cite sources.
- **Talk by voice.** Hands-free conversation with speech-to-text
  and read-aloud.
- **Connect via API.** Self-service API keys with their own budgets
  for your scripts and apps.

## Who NUFI is for

NUFI is designed around three audiences:

- **Knowledge workers** who want a single AI workspace for their
  day-to-day work — research, writing, coding, analysis.
- **Developers** who want to call modern AI from their own code
  without managing infrastructure or juggling provider keys.
- **Admins** responsible for AI adoption inside an organisation —
  who can use what models, how much each team can spend, what
  features are turned on for whom.

## What makes NUFI different

| | Public AI products | NUFI |
|---|---|---|
| **Account model** | Per-user paid SaaS | Centralised per-organisation |
| **Budget control** | Hidden in pricing | Visible, per-user and per-team caps |
| **Model choice** | Locked to vendor | Any provider, any model |
| **Audit & visibility** | None | Every request logged, attributable |
| **API access** | Separate billing | Same account, same budget |
| **Privacy** | Vendor's terms apply | You choose what leaves your boundary |

## The two surfaces

You will use one or both:

- **The app** — `chat.nufi.me`. Where day-to-day conversations
  happen. This is what end users see.
- **The console** — `console.nufi.me`. Where developers manage
  their own API keys, watch their usage, and tune their limits.
  Same login as the chat.

Admins also have a third surface — the **admin panel** at
`admin.nufi.me` — for managing the whole platform. End users do not
need to know about it.
