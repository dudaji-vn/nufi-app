A department buys one box. On it sit a shared drive, a chat tool and an
agent runtime. The drive already holds the work; the question is what to
point the agent at first.

These ten flows are that answer. Each one is small enough to read in a
minute and open enough to edit. Every question and answer below is a real
run against a box with an on-box model — nothing here is an illustration.

<Callout>
Build them into your own Studio with
`deploy/platform/scenarios/studio/build_flows.py`, then run them all with
`run_flows.py`. The second script prints exactly what the flows returned,
which is where the answers on this page come from.
</Callout>

## The ten

| # | Team | What the flow does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal | Reviews a draft clause against the review guide and names the article that forbids it |
| 2 | HR | Answers a leave question by **calling a calculator**, not by doing the sum itself |
| 3 | General Affairs | Turns one line of instruction into a notice in the house format |
| 4 | Corporate Strategy | Turns meeting notes into decisions with an owner and a date |
| 5 | Finance | Picks the duplicate-payment suspects out of a payment list |
| 6 | Sales | Breaks an RFP paragraph into a requirement checklist |
| 7 | Customer Support | Grades tickets by priority and states the rule it used |
| 8 | Engineering | Turns an incident log into a postmortem stub |
| 9 | Any team | Reduces last night's mail to what needs answering today |
| 10 | Any team | Tags a batch of files by owning team and retention |

Four are shown in full below. The rest follow the same shape.

## 1 — Legal: auto-review of risky clauses

Four components: the question, the department's review guide, the on-box
model, the answer. The guide is the **only** thing the flow may judge
against, which is what stops it inventing a rule that sounds plausible.

**Asked**

> 다음 조항을 검토해줘: '본 계약의 손해배상 책임에는 상한을 두지 아니한다.'
> 그리고 '경업금지 기간은 5년으로 한다.'

**Answered**

```
(1) 위험 여부: 위험합니다.
(2) 근거 조항 번호: 제6조, 제3조
(3) 해야 할 조치: 무제한 손해배상 조항 삭제 및 경업금지 기간 3년 이내로 수정.
```

Both clauses caught, each against the article that forbids it.

## 2 — HR: leave entitlement, computed by a tool

This one is worth reading even if your team is not HR, because of what it
avoids.

The company rule is *15 days after one full year, plus one day for every
two years of continuous service beyond the first, capped at 25*. Asked to
apply that to three years of service, the model on its own answers **20**
— confidently, and at temperature zero it answers 20 every time. The
correct figure is 16.

So the flow does not let it. A calculator is handed to the agent as a
tool, and the system prompt forbids doing the arithmetic directly:

```
ChatInput ─┐
Prompt ────┼─▶ ToolCallingAgent ─▶ ChatOutput
On-box model (LanguageModel) ─┘        ▲
                        CalculatorTool ┘
```

**Asked** — 3년 근속한 직원의 연차휴가는 며칠인가요?

**Answered**

```
3년 근속한 직원의 연차휴가는 16일입니다.
```

The Playground shows the `CALCULATOR` calls that produced it.

<Callout type="warn">
The general lesson: retrieval grounds **finding**, not **reasoning**. A
model reading the right policy will still derive the wrong number from
it. Anything that must be *computed* — entitlements, prorations,
deadlines, totals — belongs in a tool, where the answer is arithmetic
rather than a guess.
</Callout>

## 4 — Corporate Strategy: meeting notes to decisions

**Asked** — 회의 메모: 8월 30일 전략기획 회의. 김대리가 9월 5일까지 파일럿
후보 3곳 선정. 이과장이 9월 10일까지 제안서 초안. 예산 재검토는 논의만 하고
담당자를 정하지 못함.

**Answered**

| 결정사항 | 담당자 | 기한 |
|---|---|---|
| 파일럿 후보 3곳 선정 | 김대리 | 9월 5일까지 |
| 제안서 초안 작성 | 이과장 | 9월 10일까지 |
| 예산 재검토 논의 | 미지정 | 미지정 |

The third row is the point. The notes never assigned it, so the flow
writes `미지정` instead of picking someone. A summariser that fills that
cell to look complete is worse than no summariser.

## 7 — Customer Support: ticket triage

**Asked** — 티켓 1: 전 직원이 박스에 접속하지 못함. 티켓 2: 공유 드라이브만
안 보임. 티켓 3: 화면 글씨를 키워달라는 요청.

**Answered**

```
티켓 1 / P1 / 30분 이내 / 서비스 전체 중단에 해당.
티켓 2 / P2 / 4시간 이내 / 주요 기능 장애에 해당.
티켓 3 / P4 / 3영업일 이내 / 문의·개선 요청에 해당.
```

Each grade cites the rule it came from, so a disagreement is about the
standard rather than about the model.

## What these flows deliberately do not do

**They do not mask personal data.** The product introduction sells PII
masking, and it is a real feature — but it belongs to the guardrail layer,
not to a prompt. A prompt asked to mask a Korean document left an email
address untouched and dropped the account number entirely. Deterministic
detectors handle this; a model asked nicely does not.

**They do not answer outside their material.** Each system prompt names
what the flow may use and tells it to say so when the answer is not
there. That is why the Strategy flow writes `미지정` and the Sales flow
writes `조건부` rather than guessing.

**They are pinned, not sampled.** Temperature is zero and `top_k` is one.
Left sampling, the same Korean question came back finished in Chinese —
the notice-drafting flow produced `정보资产安全及保密性을`. Greedy
decoding removes the reach for those tokens, and makes a run repeatable
enough to put in a document like this one.
