---
title: Generate and iterate on images
description: Create images from a description and refine them across a few turns.
---

Describe what you want and NUFI can generate an image for you — then change it
by describing the edit, keeping every version along the way.

## What you'll need

- Image generation enabled for your organisation. (If you don't see an image
  tool, ask your admin.)

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
Turn on the image tool from the **Tools** menu (or type `/image` at the start
of the box if your deployment uses that).

![The Tools menu](/screenshots/chat-tools-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Describe what you want — subject, style, and lighting all help:

> A watercolour fox reading a book in a cosy library, soft pastel colours,
> dawn lighting.
</Step>
<Step>
NUFI returns the image in the conversation and saves it to your account.
</Step>
<Step>
Edit it by describing the change in your next message — you keep both versions:

> Make the sky purple and add a moon.
</Step>
<Step>Download the image, or keep iterating in the same chat.</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

Generated images and their iterations, saved to your account under the avatar
menu → **My Files**.

## Make it better

- **Be specific** about style and lighting — vague prompts give generic
  results.
- **Iterate in small steps** — change one thing at a time.
- **Try different models** — each produces a different look.
