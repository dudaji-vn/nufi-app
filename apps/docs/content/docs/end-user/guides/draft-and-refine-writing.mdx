---
title: Draft, refine, and translate writing
description: Go from a rough brief to a polished, translated piece — using replies, branches, and a saved prompt you can reuse.
---

Writing with NUFI works best as a conversation: draft, react, try an
alternative, translate, and save what worked so next time is one click. This
guide walks that loop end to end.

## What you'll need

- Nothing special — any model works. A stronger model helps for tone and
  nuance.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
Describe what you want in plain language:

> Draft a 150-word product-update email about feature X, friendly tone,
> ending with a clear call to action.
</Step>
<Step>
Refine by replying — you don't need to start over:

> Make it shorter and lead with the benefit, not the feature.
</Step>
<Step>
Want to try a different angle without losing this one? **Edit** your message or
**branch** the conversation to explore a second tone in parallel, then compare.

![A conversation you can edit and branch](/screenshots/chat-conversation.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Translate the version you like:

> Translate the final version into Vietnamese, keep it professional.
</Step>
<Step>
Reuse it later. Save your instruction as a **saved prompt** (managed under
**Prompts** in the left icon rail), or save the whole
`{ model, parameters, instructions }` bundle as a **preset**.

![The Presets menu](/screenshots/chat-presets-menu.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

A finished, translated draft — plus a reusable prompt or preset so the next
"product-update email" takes seconds instead of minutes.

## Make it better

- **Show the tone you want** — paste a short example; the AI matches it.
- **Keep versions** by branching instead of overwriting — you can always go
  back.
- **Share what works** — a good prompt or agent is worth giving to the whole
  team; see the automation guide below.
