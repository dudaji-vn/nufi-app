---
title: Draft, refine, and translate writing
description: Go from a rough brief to a polished, translated piece, and save what worked so next time is one click.
---

Writing with NUFI works best as a conversation: draft, react, try an
alternative, translate, and save what worked. This guide walks that loop
end to end. The first four steps work in Basic mode; saving a prompt or
preset needs [Advanced](/docs/end-user/advanced-mode).

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
Describe what you want in plain language:

> Draft a 150-word product-update email about feature X, friendly tone,
> ending with a clear call to action.
</Step>
<Step>
Refine by replying. You do not need to start over:

> Make it shorter and lead with the benefit, not the feature.
</Step>
<Step>
Want a second angle without losing this one? Click the pencil on your
message, change the brief, and click **Save & Submit**. Both versions are
kept, and the arrows under the message switch between them. In Advanced
mode, **Fork** under a reply does the same for a whole conversation.

![A conversation you can edit and branch](/screenshots/chat-conversation.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Translate the version you like:

> Translate the final version into Vietnamese, keep it professional.
</Step>
<Step>
Reuse it. In Advanced mode, save the instruction as a **saved prompt**
under **Prompts** in the left icon rail, or save the whole model-and-
settings combination as a **preset**.

![The Presets menu](/screenshots/chat-presets-menu.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## Make it better

- **Show the tone you want** — paste a short example and the model
  matches it.
- **Keep versions** by editing with Save & Submit rather than rewriting
  in place.
- **Share what works** — a good prompt or agent belongs to the whole
  team; see the automation guide below.
