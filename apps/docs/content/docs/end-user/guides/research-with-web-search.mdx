---
title: Research a topic with web search
description: Get up-to-date, source-cited answers by letting NUFI search the live web, then drill into the sources.
---

By default the AI answers from what it learned during training, which has a
cutoff date. **Web search** lets it look things up live and answer with
citations you can open and verify — so you can research a topic and trust the
sources.

## What you'll need

- Web search enabled for your organisation — you'll see **Web Search** in the
  **Tools** menu. (If it's missing, ask your admin.)

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>Start a **new chat**.</Step>
<Step>
Open the **Tools** menu (the sliders icon next to the paperclip) and turn on
**Web Search**. It stays on for the rest of the conversation.

![The Tools menu with Web Search](/screenshots/chat-tools-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Ask a question that needs current information, for example:

> What changed in the EU AI Act timeline this year, and when do the main
> obligations start?
</Step>
<Step>
NUFI turns your question into searches, reads the top results, and answers with
inline **citations** — small numbered links back to each source.
</Step>
<Step>
Drill in and narrow:

> Focus on obligations for small companies.

> Read source [2] more carefully — what does it say about deadlines?
</Step>
<Step>
Optional: attach one of your own files and ask NUFI to **compare** it against
what it found on the web.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

An answer backed by links you can check yourself — a paragraph with `[1] [2]`
citations you hover to preview and click to open the original page.

## Make it better

- **Ask for sources explicitly** — add *"with sources"* or *"cite each claim"*.
- **Be specific** — *"Compare the 2025 camera reviews of phone A vs phone B"*
  beats *"which phone is better?"*.
- **Cross-check** anything important before you rely on it.
- **Privacy:** whatever the AI looks up becomes a search query that leaves NUFI
  for the search provider — turn search **off** for confidential prompts.
