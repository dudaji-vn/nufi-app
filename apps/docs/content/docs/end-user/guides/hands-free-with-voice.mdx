---
title: Go hands-free with voice
description: Dictate your messages and have replies read back to you, with nothing to set up.
---

You do not have to type. The app transcribes what you say into the
message box and can read replies aloud, using your browser's built-in
speech engine. Nothing has to be enabled by your organisation.

## What you will need

- A browser with speech recognition. Chrome and Edge have it; Safari and
  Firefox only partly.
- Microphone permission, which the browser asks for the first time.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>Start or continue a chat.</Step>
<Step>Click the **microphone** at the bottom-right of the message box and
speak. Click it again to stop; the transcript appears in the box.</Step>
<Step>Read it over, then press **Enter**.</Step>
<Step>Click the **speaker** under a reply to hear it.</Step>
<Step>
For a real conversation, open **Settings → Speech** and turn on
**Conversation Mode**: you speak, it sends, it answers aloud, it listens
again. **Voice** and **Audio Playback Rate** on the same tab change how
replies sound.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Make it better

- **A quiet room** and short sentences with pauses transcribe best.
- **Set the dictation language** on the Speech tab if it keeps mishearing
  you.
- **Keep Auto send text off** when the content is sensitive, so you can
  read the transcript before it goes.
