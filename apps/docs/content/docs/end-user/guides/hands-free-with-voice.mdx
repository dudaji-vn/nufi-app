---
title: Go hands-free with voice
description: Speak your messages and have replies read back to you.
---

You don't have to type. NUFI can transcribe what you say into the message box
(speech-to-text) and read replies aloud (text-to-speech) — handy on the move or
for accessibility.

## What you'll need

- Speech enabled for your organisation. (If the controls aren't there, ask your
  admin.)
- Microphone permission in your browser — it'll ask the first time.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>Start or continue a chat.</Step>
<Step>
Use the **microphone** control in the message box to dictate. Your speech is
transcribed into the box as you talk.
</Step>
<Step>Review the transcribed text, then send it as usual.</Step>
<Step>
Turn on **read-aloud** to hear replies spoken back to you instead of reading
them.
</Step>
<Step>
If your deployment offers a choice, pick a voice or speech engine in settings.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

Hands-free chatting — dictate a question while doing something else, and listen
to the answer.

## Make it better

- **A quiet room** helps transcription accuracy.
- **Keep utterances short** and pause between thoughts.
- **Pick a natural-sounding voice** if your deployment offers more than one.
