---
title: Set up a team workspace
description: Create a team, invite people, set roles, and share knowledge, agents, and prompts — to everyone or a group.
---

A team is a shared workspace. One person sets up the knowledge, agents, and
prompts once, and everyone works from the same source. This guide takes you
from an empty team to a fully shared workspace.

## What you'll need

- Permission to create teams. (If you don't see the **Teams** icon, ask your
  admin.)

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Teams** (the people icon in the left icon rail) and click **Create
Team**. Give it a name and an optional description — you become the **Owner**.

![The Create Team dialog](/screenshots/chat-team-create.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Go to the **Members** tab and click **Invite member**. Enter an email address
and pick a role (**Member** or **Admin**). They accept the invitation inside
the app — no email link needed.

![The Invite member dialog](/screenshots/chat-team-invite.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Manage people from the **Members** tab: change roles, transfer ownership, or
remove someone. Give each person the least role they need.

![The Members tab with role controls](/screenshots/chat-team-members.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Share knowledge: open the **Knowledge** tab, click **Add file**, and choose who
it's for — the whole team or a group. Now everyone can ask questions answered
from those documents.

![Sharing a knowledge file](/screenshots/chat-team-knowledge.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Share assistants and prompts from the **Shared** tab — an **Add agent** and an
**Add prompt** button, each with its own audience.

![The Shared tab](/screenshots/chat-team-shared.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Need to share with only *some* of the team? Create a **group** on the **Groups**
tab, add members, and target that group when you share.

![The Groups tab](/screenshots/chat-team-groups.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

A shared workspace where the whole team asks the same knowledge, uses the same
assistants, and reuses the same prompts — no duplicated uploads, no drift.

## Make it better

- **Use groups** for sensitive material (a `Manager`-only policy) while a
  general handbook stays shared with everyone.
- **Least privilege** — most people only need **Member**.
- **Transfer ownership** before you leave; only the Owner can delete the team.
