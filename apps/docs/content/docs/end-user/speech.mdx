---
title: Speech
description: Talk to the app hands-free and have replies read aloud.
---

NUFI can turn your voice into a message and read replies back out
loud — handy when your hands are busy, you're on the move, or typing
is uncomfortable.

## Voice in — talk instead of type

<Steps>
<Step>Find the **microphone** icon at the bottom-right of the message
box (just left of the send arrow).</Step>
<Step>Tap it once to start recording, tap again to stop. Your words
appear as text in the message box.</Step>
<Step>Edit the text if you need to, then press **Enter** to send.</Step>
</Steps>

If there's no microphone icon, your organisation hasn't connected a
voice-to-text engine — ask your admin.

## Voice out — have replies read aloud

Click the **🔊 speaker** icon in the toolbar under any reply (see
[the answer toolbar](/docs/end-user/chat#the-answer-toolbar)). The
reply is read aloud as it streams in.

## Hands-free mode

Open the avatar menu (bottom-left) → **Settings → Speech** to enable:

- **Auto-send** — when you stop talking, the message sends
  automatically. No tap needed.
- **Auto-play** — every reply is read aloud as it arrives.

Turn on both for true hands-free use — you talk, NUFI answers, you
talk again. Great for the car, a walk, or cooking.

## Languages

Voice input detects the language you speak automatically. Voice output
uses the app's language (Settings → General → Language). Most major
languages work out of the box — English, Vietnamese, Chinese,
Japanese, Korean, and most European languages.

## A note on privacy

Voice in and out send audio out of your deployment to a speech
provider. For anything sensitive, type it instead — or keep auto-send
off so you can review the text before sending.

## Common issues

- **Microphone permission denied** — your browser is blocking the mic.
  Click the lock icon in the address bar and allow microphone access.
- **Audio sounds robotic** — that's the speech engine. Some
  organisations connect a higher-quality one; ask your admin.
- **Voice output cuts off** — your network dropped briefly. NUFI picks
  up from where it stopped on the next reply.
