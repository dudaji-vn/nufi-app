title: Speech
description: Dictate a message instead of typing it, and have replies read aloud. It works out of the box in a modern browser.

NUFI can turn your voice into a message and read replies back to you.
Nothing has to be set up by your organisation: by default the app uses
the speech engine built into your browser.

> **Guide:** [Go hands-free with voice](/docs/end-user/guides/hands-free-with-voice).

## Voice in

<Steps>
<Step>Click the **microphone** at the bottom-right of the message box,
just left of the send arrow. Your browser asks for microphone
permission the first time.</Step>
<Step>Speak. Click the microphone again to stop. Your words appear as
text in the box.</Step>
<Step>Fix anything it misheard, then press **Enter**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Voice out

Click the **speaker** under any reply (see
[the answer toolbar](/docs/end-user/chat#the-answer-toolbar)) and it is
read aloud.

## Hands-free

Open the avatar menu → **Settings → Speech**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto send text** | Sends the message as soon as you stop speaking |
| **Autoplay Latest Message** | Reads every reply aloud as it arrives |
| **Conversation Mode** | Both at once, in a loop: you speak, it answers, it listens again |
| **Engine** | Which speech engine to use, for dictation and for reading. **Browser** is the default |
| **Voice** | The voice replies are read in |
| **Language** | The language you dictate in |
| **Audio Playback Rate** | Faster or slower reading |

Turn on **Conversation Mode** for true hands-free use.

## Languages

Dictation uses the language set on the Speech tab, and reading uses the
voice chosen there — not the app's display language. Most browsers offer
English, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Chinese and the major European
languages.

## Privacy

With the default **Browser** engine, your audio goes wherever your
browser sends it for recognition; in Chrome that is Google's speech
service. If your organisation has connected its own engine, the audio
goes there instead. For anything sensitive, type it, or keep **Auto send
text** off so you can read the transcript before it goes.

## Common issues

- **No microphone icon** — your browser does not support speech
  recognition. Chrome and Edge do; Safari and Firefox only partly.
- **Permission denied** — click the lock icon in the address bar and
  allow the microphone.
- **It sounds robotic** — that is the browser's built-in voice. Pick a
  different one under **Voice**, or ask your administrator whether a
  better engine is available.
