---
title: Web search
description: Let the AI look up live information from the internet.
---

By default, the AI answers from what it learned during training —
which has a cutoff date and no knowledge of your specific situation.
**Web search** lets it look things up live before replying.

## Turn it on

In the message-box toolbar, toggle the **🌐 Search** chip. It stays
on for the rest of the conversation. Toggle off when you want a
purely from-training answer.

## What changes when search is on

Behind the scenes:

1. The AI extracts a search query (sometimes several) from your
   prompt.
2. It searches the web and fetches the top results.
3. It re-ranks the snippets by relevance.
4. The most useful pieces are included in its answer.
5. The reply includes inline **citations** linking back to the
   source.

You see the citations as small numbered links. Hover one to see the
exact snippet that influenced the answer.

## When to use it

Web search shines for:

- **Latest news, prices, events** — anything that changes faster
  than the AI's training data.
- **Specific facts** — exact dates, current versions, real-time
  stats.
- **Live research** — "What are people saying about X this week?"
- **Citations needed** — you want links to back up the answer.

It is less useful for:

- Brainstorming or creative writing (slows you down).
- Pure reasoning, math, code (no external info needed).
- Anything where you already provided the source material in your
  prompt.

## Tips for better results

- **Be specific in the prompt** — "Compare iPhone 16 Pro to Galaxy
  S25 Ultra on camera reviews from 2025" beats "Which phone is
  better?".
- **Ask for citations explicitly** — "with sources" or "cite each
  claim" pushes the model to attribute its answer.
- **Ask follow-ups** — once it has the search results, you can drill
  in: "Read source [2] more carefully, what does it say about
  battery life?"

## Privacy

Anything the AI needs to search for becomes part of a search query,
which leaves NUFI for the search provider. **Do not** put confidential
content into a search-enabled prompt. Toggle search off for those.

## If the toggle is missing

Your organisation has not connected a search provider. Ask your
admin to enable it.

## Cost

Search-augmented replies cost a bit more — extra fetches plus a
longer prompt. You will see the difference on the
[Usage](/docs/end-user/api-keys) page if you query a lot. For most
users it is well within budget.
