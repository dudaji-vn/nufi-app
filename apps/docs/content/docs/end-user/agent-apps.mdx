Beyond the assistants you build inside a conversation, NUFI has two
separate apps for working with agents. They solve different problems,
and the names are the fastest way to tell them apart: one is a place to
**build** something, the other is a place to **run** work.

| | **NUFI Studio** | **NUFI Works** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A canvas | An operations app |
| You use it to | Wire a model, a knowledge base and a tool into a flow, then run it | Give a team of agents a goal, approve what matters, watch the spend |
| You get out | A pipeline you can publish as an API endpoint | Completed tasks, with a record of who approved what |
| Think of it as | A workbench | A team |

Neither replaces the assistants in the chat app. Those stay where they
are — see [Agents and skills](/docs/end-user/agents).

## Opening them

Both live behind one door.

<Steps>
<Step>Go to `agents.nufi.me`. Sign in to the NUFI app first if you have
not already — the door needs a session to know who you are.</Step>
<Step>Pick **NUFI Studio** or **NUFI Works**.</Step>
<Step>You land inside the app, already signed in.</Step>
</Steps>

There is no **Agents** button inside the chat app yet — bookmark the
address for now.

## Why there is no second password

There is no separate account for Studio or for Works. Your NUFI account
is the account, and the sign-in you already did is the one that counts.

When you pick a product, NUFI vouches for you to it: it confirms who you
are, says what you are allowed to do, and hands that over for a limited
time. The product creates your user the first time it sees you and
recognises you from then on.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- **Signing out of NUFI does not instantly sign you out of Studio or
  Works.** What was handed over stays valid until it expires — up to
  eight hours by default. On a shared machine, close the tabs.
- **Everyone with a NUFI account can open both.** There is no separate
  invite list. If your organisation needs one, that is a setting an
  administrator has to ask for; it does not exist yet.

<Callout type="warn">
Only ever reach these apps from the NUFI app or by typing the address
yourself. A link from elsewhere that lands you on a page asking to
"continue with NUFI" is worth a second look before you click it.
</Callout>

## Which one do I want?

Ask what you have in your hand.

- **A question and some documents** — you want neither of these. Use
  the chat app; see [Chat with a document](/docs/end-user/guides/chat-with-a-document).
- **A repeatable process** — extract fields from an invoice, answer
  from a document set, summarise every incoming ticket — that is a flow.
  Use **Studio**.
- **A body of work with several moving parts and a deadline** — that is
  a team with a goal. Use **Works**, and start with
  [Assigning work to agents](/docs/end-user/agent-tasks).

## Costs

Everything either app does with a model runs through NUFI and lands on
your usage, the same as a conversation does. You can see it in the
console under [API keys](/docs/end-user/api-keys).

In Works there is one extra step before that is true for you
specifically — see
[Connecting your NUFI account](/docs/end-user/agent-connect). Until you
do it, agents run on whatever key the server was configured with.
