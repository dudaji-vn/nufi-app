title: Models and presets
description: Choose which model answers, tune how it replies, and save the combination as a preset.
---

NUFI offers several models. You choose which one answers each
conversation, and in Advanced mode you can shape how it replies and
save that as a preset.

> **Guide:** [Draft, refine, and translate writing](/docs/end-user/guides/draft-and-refine-writing)
> uses presets and saved prompts.

## Pick a model

<Steps>
<Step>Click the **model name** at the top-left of the conversation — in
the screenshot below it reads *gemini*.</Step>
<Step>The menu lists the model groups available to you. A group with a
chevron opens into its models; **Search models…** filters the list.</Step>
<Step>Click a model. The conversation uses it from the next message on.</Step>
</Steps>

![The model picker open, showing the available model groups](/screenshots/chat-model-menu.png)

In Basic mode the picker shows a short curated list; Advanced shows
every group, and any agents you have built appear there too. When your
administrator adds a model, reloading the page is enough to see it.

Larger models are usually better at reasoning and slower; smaller ones
are quicker and cheaper. Try a few on the kind of question you ask most.

## Tune the response style

<Callout>
The Parameters panel is in [Advanced mode](/docs/end-user/advanced-mode)
only, and only for plain models — an agent carries its own settings.
</Callout>

Open **Parameters** from the left icon rail:

![The Parameters panel with model settings](/screenshots/chat-parameters.png)

The settings you will actually reach for:

- **Custom Instructions** — what the model should know before your
  first message: a persona, a tone, standing rules. *("You are a concise
  assistant. Reply in Vietnamese.")*
- **Temperature** — low, near 0, for precise and repeatable answers
  (code, summaries, data); high, near 1, for varied and creative ones.
- **Max Output Tokens** — caps how long a single reply can be.

The rest — Top P, frequency and presence penalty, reasoning effort — are
sampling controls. Leave them alone unless you know what you want.
**Reset Model Parameters** at the bottom restores the defaults.

Settings apply to the current conversation only. Save a combination you
like as a preset.

## Presets

A preset is a saved bundle of model, parameters and instructions under a
name. **Presets** in the top bar lists yours — *"No presets yet, use the
settings button to create one"* until you make one.

![The Presets menu](/screenshots/chat-presets-menu.png)

- **Create one** — set a model and its parameters the way you like, then
  save from the Parameters panel under a clear name: *Code review*,
  *Translation EN→VN*, *Friendly reply*.
- **Use one** — pick it from the menu. A new conversation starts with
  all its settings applied.
- **Import** and **Clear all** are in the same menu.

## Saved prompts

A saved prompt is lighter than a preset: a piece of text you reuse,
with no model attached. Manage them under **Prompts** in the left icon
rail, and insert one by typing `/` at the start of the message box.
Good candidates are personas, boilerplate, and tone modifiers like
*"Rewrite in plain English, no jargon."*

## If the model menu is empty

- In Basic mode the list is curated, and it can be empty if nobody has
  curated one yet. Switch to Advanced, or ask your administrator.
- Reload the page once.
- If it is still empty, the platform cannot reach its model gateway.
  Your administrators have monitoring that says why.
