Type a question, press **Enter**, and the answer streams back. This page
walks through the rest, click by click.

![A conversation in the NUFI app, with the answer toolbar under the reply](/screenshots/chat-conversation.png)

> **Guides:** [Draft, refine, and translate writing](/docs/end-user/guides/draft-and-refine-writing)
> and [Chat with a document](/docs/end-user/guides/chat-with-a-document).

## Send your first message

<Steps>
<Step>Open `chat.nufi.me`. You land on a new chat with an empty box at
the bottom that says **Message**, followed by the name of the model or
agent that will answer — *Nufi* in the screenshots here.</Step>
<Step>Type a question — *"In one sentence, what can you help me with?"*</Step>
<Step>Press **Enter** to send. **Shift + Enter** adds a line instead.</Step>
<Step>The reply streams in under the assistant's name. That is all there
is to it.</Step>
</Steps>

While a reply is streaming, **Stop** cancels it. Afterwards,
**Regenerate** asks for a different answer to the same message.

## The answer toolbar

Hover a reply and a row of small buttons appears under it, left to
right:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Speaker | Reads the reply aloud — see [Speech](/docs/end-user/speech) |
| Copy | Copies the reply |
| Edit | Changes the message and, if you want, asks again |
| Fork | Starts a new conversation from this point *(Advanced mode)* |
| Thumbs up / down | Rates the answer for your administrators |
| Regenerate | Asks for a fresh answer |
| Continue | Appears when a reply stopped early; asks the model to carry on |

## Edit and try again

Click the pencil on a message *you* sent and change the wording. Then:

- **Save & Submit** sends the edited message and gets a new reply. The
  original stays: small arrows under the message let you switch between
  the two versions, so you can compare answers to two phrasings.
- **Save** just fixes the text of the message without asking again.

## Your conversations

The left sidebar lists your chats under **Today**, **Yesterday**,
**Previous 7 days** and **Previous 30 days**. The pencil-and-square icon
at the top starts a **New chat**; **Search messages** finds a
conversation by any word you remember from it.

Hover a conversation for its menu: **Rename**, **Duplicate**,
**Archive**, **Delete**. Archived chats leave the list but are kept —
**Settings → General → Archived chats** lists them and brings one back.

## Temporary chat

**Temporary Chat** at the top-right starts a conversation that is not
saved. Close it and it is gone. Use it for one-off questions you do not
want in your history.

## Share or export a conversation

**Share** in the top bar creates a link to the conversation. Anyone in
your organisation who is signed in can open it; the link does not work
for people outside. Your name, and anything you add after sharing, stay
private. **Settings → Data controls → Shared links** lists every link
you have made and lets you delete one.

**Export**, in the same menu, saves the conversation as a file in the
format you choose.

## In Advanced mode

These appear once you switch to [Advanced](/docs/end-user/advanced-mode):

- **Add multi-conversation** in the top bar opens a second conversation
  beside the first — the quickest way to compare two models on one
  question.
- **Bookmarks**: the tag icon in the top bar puts a named tag on the
  conversation, and the **Bookmarks** panel in the rail lists them.
- **Fork**, under any message, starts a new conversation that keeps
  everything up to that point.
- `/` at the start of the message box inserts a saved prompt — see
  [Models and presets](/docs/end-user/models-and-presets#saved-prompts).

## Limits

The app is shared across your organisation, so requests are
rate-limited. If you send very quickly you get *"Too many requests. Try
again later"* — wait a few seconds. Spend is measured centrally by your
administrators; nothing in the app asks you to top up.
