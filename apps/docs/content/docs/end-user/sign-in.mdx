## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step>Open `chat.nufi.me`.</Step>
<Step>Click **Sign up** (under *"Don't have an account?"*).</Step>
<Step>Enter your name, email, and a strong password, then submit.</Step>
</Steps>

Depending on how your organisation set NUFI up, you might instead see
a **Continue with Google** (or Microsoft / company SSO) button. Use
that — you won't need a separate password. If neither option appears,
your administrator creates accounts for everyone; ask them.

## Sign in

On the **Welcome back** screen, enter your **email address** and
**password** and click **Continue** — or use **Continue with Google**
if your organisation set up single sign-on.

Once signed in, you stay signed in for several days. You also get into
the **console** (`console.nufi.me`) automatically — no second login.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor (**2FA**) adds a code from your phone on top of your
password. Strongly recommended.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the **avatar menu** at the **bottom-left** → **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Go to the **Account** section and click **Enable two-factor**.</Step>
<Step>Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google
Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator — any work).</Step>
<Step>Type the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.</Step>
<Step>**Save the backup codes** somewhere safe — you'll need them if
you lose your phone.</Step>
</Steps>

Next time you sign in, NUFI asks for the 6-digit code after your
password.

## Reset your password

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. If your organisation
has email delivery configured, a reset link arrives within a minute.
If it doesn't, ask your administrator to reset it for you.

## Your account menu

Click your **avatar at the bottom-left** of the app to open the
account menu:

![The account menu — My Files, Help & FAQ, Console, Settings, Log out](/screenshots/chat-account-menu.png)

From here you can open **My Files**, jump to the **Console** to manage
[API keys](/docs/end-user/api-keys), open **Settings** (display name,
avatar, password, two-factor, active sessions), or **Log out**.

## Sign out

Avatar menu (bottom-left) → **Log out**. This signs you out everywhere
your account is open.

## Common issues

- **"Registration is disabled"** — your organisation only allows
  admin-created accounts. Ask your admin to invite you.
- **"Loop between chat and console"** — sign out fully, clear cookies
  for `nufi.me`, then sign in again at the app.
- **2FA code rejected** — your phone clock and the server clock are
  out of sync. Open your phone settings → Date & Time → toggle
  **Set automatically** on.
- **Forgot the email you used** — your admin can tell you (or merge
  duplicate accounts).
