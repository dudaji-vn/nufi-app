## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step>Open `chat.nufi.me`.</Step>
<Step>Click **Sign up**, under *"Don't have an account?"*.</Step>
<Step>Enter your name, email address and a password, and submit. You
are signed in straight away.</Step>
</Steps>

Or click **Continue with Google** and skip the password. A Google
account and an email account with the same address are two different
accounts, so pick one way and keep to it.

## Sign in

On the **Welcome back** screen, enter your email address and password
and click **Continue**, or use **Continue with Google**.

You stay signed in for a while after that, and the same session opens
the **console** at `console.nufi.me` and the agent apps behind
`agents.nufi.me`. There is no second login.

## Forgot your password?

`chat.nufi.me` sends no email, so there is no reset link on the sign-in
screen. Ask your administrator to reset the password for you. If you
signed up with Google there is nothing to reset: use Google's own
recovery.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from your phone on top
of your password. It is available for accounts with a password; a Google
account is protected by Google's own second factor instead.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the **avatar menu** at the bottom-left and choose **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>On the **Account** tab, click **Enable 2FA**.</Step>
<Step>Scan the QR code with an authenticator app — Google Authenticator,
1Password, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator all work.</Step>
<Step>Type the six-digit code from the app to confirm.</Step>
<Step>**Save the backup codes** somewhere safe. They are what gets you
in if you lose the phone. You can regenerate them later from the same
tab.</Step>
</Steps>

From then on, sign-in asks for the six-digit code after your password.

## The account menu

Click your **avatar** at the bottom-left of the app:

![The account menu — My Files, Help & FAQ, Console, Agents, Settings, Advanced, Log out](/screenshots/chat-agents-menu.png)

| Entry | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **My Files** | Every file you have uploaded, in any conversation or agent |
| **Help & FAQ** | The help site of the chat engine NuFi is built on |
| **Console** | Your developer console, for [API keys](/docs/end-user/api-keys) |
| **Agents** | The chooser for [Studio and Works](/docs/end-user/agent-apps) |
| **Settings** | The tabs below |
| **Advanced** / **Basic** | Switches the interface mode — see [Basic and Advanced mode](/docs/end-user/advanced-mode) |
| **Log out** | Signs you out on this device |

## Settings

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **General** | **Interface** (Basic or Advanced), **Theme**, **Language**, a few display toggles, and **Archived chats** |
| **Chat**, **Commands** | Advanced mode only: how messages render, and which `/` commands are active |
| **Speech** | Dictation and read-aloud — see [Speech](/docs/end-user/speech) |
| **Personalization** | What the assistant is allowed to remember about you, where memories are enabled |
| **Data controls** | Import conversations, **Shared links**, **Revoke Keys**, delete the speech cache, **Clear all chats** |
| **Account** | Display username in messages, profile picture, 2FA, backup codes, **Delete account** |

<Callout type="warn">
Two entries here cannot be undone. **Clear all chats** deletes every
conversation you have. **Delete account** removes the account and its
data. Neither asks your administrator first.
</Callout>

## Sign out

Avatar menu → **Log out**. That ends the session on the device you are
using. Other browsers where you are signed in stay signed in; on a
shared machine, log out there too.

## Common issues

- **I created an account with Google and now the password form rejects
  me.** The two are separate accounts. Use **Continue with Google**.
- **The 2FA code is rejected.** Your phone's clock has drifted. Turn on
  *Set automatically* in its date and time settings.
- **I am bounced between the app and the console.** Log out, clear
  cookies for `nufi.me`, and sign in again at the app.
- **I cannot find the Agents entry or the Advanced switch.** Your app
  is older than this page, or the menu was cut off on a small screen.
  Scroll the menu, or ask your administrator.
