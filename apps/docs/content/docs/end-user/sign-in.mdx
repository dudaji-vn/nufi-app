## Create your account

Open `chat.nufi.me` and click **Sign up**. Enter your name, email, and
a strong password.

Depending on how your organisation has NUFI set up, you might instead
see a **Sign in with \<provider\>** button (Google, Microsoft, your
company SSO). Use that — you do not need a separate password.

If neither option appears, your administrator creates accounts for
everyone. Ask them.

## Sign in

- **Email + password** — what you used to register.
- **Single Sign-On** — one click, no password needed if your
  organisation set it up.

Once signed in, you stay signed in for several days unless you sign
out. You also get into the **console** (`console.nufi.me`) for free —
no second login.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor (also called **2FA**) adds a code from your phone to your
login. Strongly recommended.

To enable it:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the avatar menu (top-right) → **Settings → Account**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Enable two-factor**.</Step>
<Step>Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
1Password, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator — any of them).</Step>
<Step>Type the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.</Step>
<Step>**Save the backup codes** somewhere safe. You will need them if
you lose your phone.</Step>
</Steps>

Next time you sign in, NUFI asks for the 6-digit code after your
password.

## Reset your password

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. If your organisation
has email delivery configured, you will receive a reset link within a
minute.

If you do not get the email, ask your administrator to reset it for
you.

## Sign out

Avatar menu (top-right) → **Sign out**. This signs you out everywhere
your account is open.

## Your profile

The avatar menu also has **Settings**, where you can:

- Change your display name.
- Upload an avatar photo.
- Change your password.
- Turn two-factor on or off.
- See your active sessions and end them remotely.
- Open the console to manage API keys.

## Common issues

- **"Registration is disabled"** — your organisation only allows
  admin-created accounts. Ask your admin to invite you.
- **"Loop between chat and console"** — sign out fully, clear cookies
  for `nufi.me`, then sign in again at the chat.
- **2FA code rejected** — your phone clock and the server clock are
  out of sync. Open your phone settings → Date & Time → toggle
  **Set automatically** on.
- **Forgot the email you used** — your admin can tell you (or merge
  duplicate accounts).
