title: Teams and sharing
description: Create a team, invite people, and share knowledge files, agents, and prompts — to everyone or to a specific group.
---

A **team** is a shared workspace. Instead of everyone building their own
agents and re-uploading the same documents, one person sets things up
once and shares them — so the whole team works from the same knowledge,
the same assistants, and the same prompts.

You reach teams from the **Teams** icon (the people icon) in the left
icon rail.

![The Teams page showing your teams and the Create Team button](/screenshots/chat-teams-list.png)

## What you can share

A team lets you share three kinds of things:

| You share… | So teammates can… | Where |
|------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Knowledge files** | Ask questions answered from those documents | **Knowledge** tab |
| **Agents** | Use an assistant you built, without rebuilding it | **Shared** tab |
| **Prompts** | Reuse a saved prompt | **Shared** tab |

> Shared knowledge files power **document chat** for the whole team —
> see [Knowledge and document chat](/docs/end-user/knowledge) for how
> that grounding works.

## Roles

Every member has a role. The role decides what they can change.

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything — invite, remove, change roles, share, manage groups, transfer ownership, delete the team. One owner per team. |
| **Admin** | Manage members and invites, and share knowledge / agents / prompts. |
| **Member** | Use everything shared with the team. |

## Create a team

<Steps>
<Step>Open **Teams** from the left icon rail.</Step>
<Step>Click **Create Team** (top-right).</Step>
<Step>Give it a **name** and an optional **description**, then click
**Create**. You become the team's **Owner**.</Step>
</Steps>

![The Create Team dialog with name and description](/screenshots/chat-team-create.png)

## Invite people

<Steps>
<Step>Open your team and go to the **Members** tab.</Step>
<Step>Click **Invite member**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the person's **email address** and pick a **role**
(**Member** or **Admin**), then click **Invite member**.</Step>
</Steps>

![The Invite member dialog with an email field and a role picker](/screenshots/chat-team-invite.png)

The person you invited sees the invitation **inside the app** and
accepts it to join — no email link is required. Pending invitations
you've sent appear under the **Invites** tab until they're accepted.

### Manage members

The **Members** tab lists everyone on the team. As an owner or admin
you can:

- **Change a role** — switch someone between Member and Admin with the
  role dropdown.
- **Transfer ownership** — hand the Owner role to another member.
- **Remove** — take someone off the team.

![The Members tab listing members with role controls](/screenshots/chat-team-members.png)

## Share a knowledge file

Files you share here become the team's shared **knowledge** — anyone on
the team can ask questions answered from them.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the **Knowledge** tab and click **Add file**.</Step>
<Step>Choose who it's for under **Share with** — the **whole team**, or
a specific **group** (see [Groups](#groups)).</Step>
<Step>Pick a file from your uploaded files and click **Add file**. It
now appears in the team's knowledge.</Step>
</Steps>

![Choosing a file to share and who to share it with](/screenshots/chat-team-knowledge.png)

> You share files you've **already uploaded** to your own account. To
> get a document into your account first, upload it in a chat or an
> agent — see [Files and images](/docs/end-user/files-and-images).

## Share an agent or a prompt

Open the **Shared** tab. It has two sections — **Agents** and
**Prompts** — each with its own **Add** button.

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Add agent** (or **Add prompt**).</Step>
<Step>Choose the audience under **Share with** — the whole team or a
group.</Step>
<Step>Pick the agent (or prompt) to share and confirm. Teammates can now
find it in their own agent and prompt lists.</Step>
</Steps>

![The Shared tab with shared agents and prompts](/screenshots/chat-team-shared.png)

An item's tag (e.g. **Whole team**) shows who it's shared with. Remove
sharing from the same list at any time.

## Groups

Share with just *some* of the team. Sometimes you don't want to share
with everyone. A
**group** is a named subset of the team — like `Manager` or `Staff` —
that you can target when sharing.

![The Groups tab with two groups and a New group button](/screenshots/chat-team-groups.png)

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Groups** tab, click **New group** and name it.</Step>
<Step>Use the **manage members** icon on the group to add the people who
belong to it.</Step>
<Step>When you share a file, agent, or prompt, pick that group under
**Share with** instead of *Whole team*.</Step>
</Steps>

A person can be in more than one group, and they get everything shared
with **any** group they're in (access adds up — it never takes away).
This is handy for things like a `Manager`-only policy document while a
general handbook stays shared with the whole team.

## Leaving or deleting a team

- **Members and admins** can leave a team they no longer need.
- Only the **Owner** can **delete** the team (the **Delete team**
  button, top-right of the team page). Deleting removes the team and
  un-shares everything that was shared through it — the original files,
  agents, and prompts still live in each person's own account.
- To step back without deleting, an owner can **transfer ownership** to
  someone else first.

## Troubleshooting

- **I don't see the Teams icon** — your organisation hasn't enabled
  teams for you. Ask your administrator.
- **A teammate can't see what I shared** — check the **Share with**
  audience. If you shared with a group, make sure they're in it. Newly
  shared items can take a moment to appear.
- **I can't invite or share** — those actions need the **Owner** or
  **Admin** role. Ask an owner to change your role or share on your
  behalf.
- **I invited the wrong person** — cancel the pending invite on the
  **Invites** tab, or remove them on **Members** if they already joined.
