---
title: Basic and Advanced mode
description: A new account shows a deliberately simple interface. Most of what this manual describes is one switch away.
---

The NUFI app has two interface modes, and every account starts in
**Basic**. Basic is the app as a chat window: a message box, a model, your
conversation history, files, and speech. Nothing else is on screen.

Everything else — agents, skills, saved prompts, presets, bookmarks,
teams, the Tools menu, the Parameters panel, forking a conversation — is
in **Advanced**. The features are still there; Basic simply hides them.

When you first sign in, a banner says so: *"Basic interface enabled —
switch to Advanced anytime in Settings."*

![The Basic interface: a chat window, the conversation list, and the banner](/screenshots/chat-home.png)

## Switching

Either of these, and the change is immediate:

- Open the **avatar menu** at the bottom-left and click **Advanced**.
  The same entry reads **Basic** when you are in Advanced, so it is also
  the way back.
- Open **Settings → General → Interface** and choose **Advanced**.

![Settings → General, with the Interface selector](/screenshots/chat-settings-general.png)

The setting is remembered by your browser, not your account. A
different browser, a private window, or cleared site data starts in
Basic again.

## What Advanced adds

| Where | In Advanced you also get |
|---|---|
| Left icon rail | Agent Builder, Skills, Prompts, Memories, Bookmarks, Teams, Parameters, MCP servers |
| Top bar | Presets, the bookmark tag, **Add multi-conversation** |
| Message box | The **Tools** menu (sliders icon), when you are chatting with a plain model rather than an agent |
| Under a reply | **Fork** |
| Settings | The **Chat** and **Commands** tabs |

Advanced never grants you something your administrator has not enabled.
A panel that is missing in Advanced is missing because it is off for
your organisation, not because of the mode.

## Which should I use?

Stay in Basic if you want an assistant that answers questions. Switch to
Advanced the first time a page in this manual tells you to open
something you cannot find — each such page says so at the top.
