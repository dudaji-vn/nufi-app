---
title: Welcome to the NuFi app
description: Everything you can do inside the NuFi app and your developer console, and where to start.
---

The NuFi app is your AI workspace: a private place to talk to AI models,
work with your documents, build assistants, and share them with your
team, with every message checked by security controls you never have to
think about.

You will use two web apps:

- **The app** at `chat.nufi.me` — conversations, files, agents,
  presets, teams.
- **The console** at `console.nufi.me` — only if you want to call NuFi
  from your own code. Sign in once at the app; the console reads the
  same session.

There are also two agent apps behind `agents.nufi.me` — a canvas for
building flows and an operations app for running agent teams. They
accept the session you already have; see
[Studio and Works](/docs/end-user/agent-apps).

<Callout title="One switch to know about">
A new account shows the **Basic** interface: a chat window and little
else. Agents, prompts, presets, teams and the Tools menu appear when you
switch to **Advanced** from the avatar menu. Pages that need it say so.
See [Basic and Advanced mode](/docs/end-user/advanced-mode).
</Callout>

## New here?

Start with [Sign in](/docs/end-user/sign-in), then

you need it.

## If something looks different

This manual describes `chat.nufi.me` as it is today. Your organisation
can turn features on or off centrally, so a menu item may be missing
for you even in Advanced mode. When it is, ask your administrator.
