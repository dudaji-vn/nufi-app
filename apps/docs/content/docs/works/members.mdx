title: Members and access
description: Who can create a company, how to add people, and why an invite link opened in the wrong order is a dead end.

## Who can create a company

Only an **instance administrator**. Everyone else joins a company by
invitation.

That is upstream's design, not a NUFI setting, and it is enforced in the
API rather than only hidden in the interface. A signed-in member with no
company membership and no instance-admin sees **"No company access"** —
they authenticated correctly and belong to nothing yet.

To grant instance admin: **Company → Settings → Instance → Access**,
select the person, and promote them. They need to sign in again for it to
take effect.

## Inviting someone

<Steps>
<Step>Open **Company → Settings → Invites**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Create invite** and choose a role.</Step>
<Step>Send them the `works.nufi.me/invite/…` link.</Step>
</Steps>

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Everything in Admin, plus managing members |
| `admin` | Administer the company |
| `operator` | Assign tasks *(the default)* |
| `viewer` | View company work and follow along |

<Callout type="warn">
**Tell them to sign in first, then open the link.**

Signed in, opening an invite link accepts it automatically — no button,
no form. Not signed in, the invite page shows an email and password form,
and on an instance where local sign-up is closed that form is a dead
end: they have no local account and cannot create one.

The order that works is: `agents.nufi.me` → **NUFI Works** → then the
invite link.
</Callout>

## Roles are per company

A person can hold different roles in different companies on the same
instance. Instance admin is the exception — it is instance-wide, and it
carries the ability to create companies and see everything.

## Removing someone

Removing a member from a company stops their access to that company's
work. It does not delete their account, and it does not revoke any NUFI
Studio API key they created — those are separate and outlive membership.
