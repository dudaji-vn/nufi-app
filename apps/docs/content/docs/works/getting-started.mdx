## Signing in

Works has no password of its own. Your NuFi account is the account.

![The NuFi Works sign-in page](/screenshots/works-signin.png)

<Steps>
<Step>In the NuFi app at `chat.nufi.me`, open the account menu and
choose **Agents** — or go straight to **`agents.nufi.me`**. Then pick
**NuFi Works**. You arrive signed in.</Step>
<Step>If you land on the sign-in page instead — because you typed the
address directly, or your session expired — click **Continue with
NuFi**. The email and password form below it is for local accounts,
which most instances do not issue.</Step>
</Steps>

## Creating a company

A company is the container for everything else: agents, tasks, budgets,
members. Without one you see the setup wizard and nothing else.

<Callout>
**Only an instance administrator can create a company.** Everyone else
joins one by invitation. If you see *"No company access"* after signing
in, you are authenticated but not yet a member of anything — ask for an
invite. See [Members and access](/docs/works/members).
</Callout>

The wizard is five steps.

![The first step of the setup wizard](/screenshots/works-onboarding.png)

<Steps>
<Step>**Name your company.**</Step>
<Step>**Define your mission.** Either type it, or answer a few questions
and let Works draft one. The wizard is explicit about why it asks:
*"Your mission guides everything — your lead agent, who you bring on, and
the work your company takes on."* It is not decoration; agents are shown
it alongside the task they are given.</Step>
<Step>**Create your team lead.** Works defaults to *Chief of staff*.
Rename it to whatever fits.</Step>
<Step>**Connect a model.** The wizard recommends **NuFi Agent**, which
runs on NuFi's own gateway with nothing else to set up. Take it. The
coding harnesses — Claude Code, Codex and the rest — sit behind **More
Agent Adapter Types** and need a sandbox most instances do not have.</Step>
<Step>Finish. You land on the dashboard, with two agents already
provisioned: **Reflection Coach** and **Summarizer**, both paused.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
If you picked a coding adapter anyway, the lead still exists; it just
cannot run until an operator configures a sandbox provider. Its adapter
can be changed afterwards from the agent's own page, so there is no need
to start over. See [Agents](/docs/works/agents).
</Callout>

## The dashboard

![The NuFi Works dashboard](/screenshots/works-dashboard.png)

Four numbers across the top: **Agents Enabled**, **Tasks In Progress**,
**Month Spend**, **Pending Approvals**. Below them, fourteen-day trends
for run activity, task priority, task status and success rate. On a new
company all of it reads zero, which is correct rather than broken.

The left rail groups everything by what it is for:

| Group | Holds |
|---|---|
| — | **New Task**, **Dashboard**, **Inbox**, and **Decisions** where that experimental view is switched on |
| **WORK** | Tasks, Routines, Artifacts, Skills, Projects |
| **AGENTS** | Each agent by name, and **See all agents** |
| **COMPANY** | Org, Timeline, Costs, Activity, Settings |

## The Inbox

**Inbox** is the queue of things waiting on a person: approvals that need
a decision, runs that failed, requests to join the company, alerts, and
the tasks you touched recently. When something an agent did needs you,
this is where it surfaces — the dashboard counts it, the Inbox lists it.
