The admin panel is the web app at `admin.nufi.me` where you manage
NUFI for your organisation — configuration, roles, groups, members,
and capability grants.

![The admin panel dashboard with quick links](/screenshots/admin-home.png)

## Sign in

![The admin panel sign-in screen](/screenshots/admin-sign-in.png)

<Steps>
<Step>Open `admin.nufi.me`. The admin panel does **not** auto-SSO from
the app — it has its own sign-in.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **email and password** of a NUFI account whose role is
`ADMIN`, then click **Sign in**. (If single sign-on is configured, use
that instead.)</Step>
<Step>If your account has two-factor enabled, type the 6-digit code
when prompted.</Step>
</Steps>

When SSO-only mode is enabled, the email/password form is hidden and
visitors are redirected to the identity provider.

## Find your way around

The left sidebar has five destinations:

| Section | What it's for |
|---------|---------------|
| **Dashboard** | The home screen with quick links to every area |
| **Configuration** | Every setting NUFI reads — providers, models, features, files, system. See [Configuration](/docs/admin/configuration) |
| **Access** | Define **roles** and **groups**, and assign **members**. See [Roles and groups](/docs/admin/roles-and-groups) |
| **Grants** | Choose which **capabilities** each role holds. See [Capabilities](/docs/admin/capabilities) |
| **Help** | Links to documentation and support |

Your account avatar sits at the bottom-left; the **Command menu**
(`⌘K` / search) at the top-right jumps to any page.

## The capability model

Every admin action is gated by a **capability**. You don't hand out
individual permissions — you grant a **role** a bundle of
capabilities (on the [Grants](/docs/admin/capabilities) page), then put
people into that role (on the [Access](/docs/admin/roles-and-groups)
page).

Out of the box there are two **System** roles:

- **`ADMIN`** — holds every capability (20 of them).
- **`USER`** — holds none; ordinary app access only.

To create a constrained sub-admin, make a new role, grant it just the
capabilities it needs, and add the relevant people as members.

## A typical workflow

<Steps>
<Step>A teammate needs limited admin powers. On **Access → Roles**,
click **Create role** and name it (e.g. `SUPPORT`).</Step>
<Step>On **Grants**, open that role and tick only the capabilities it
should have.</Step>
<Step>Back on **Access**, open the role's **Members** tab and add the
teammate.</Step>
<Step>Need a group to see a particular model or higher limit? Create a
**Group** under Access, add members, then add a scoped override for it
in **Configuration** (see [Scoped overrides](/docs/admin/scoped-overrides)).</Step>
</Steps>
