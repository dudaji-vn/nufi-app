# Leave Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors engaged through an agency follow their agency's terms and are out of scope.

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee is granted 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees who have completed three continuous years of service are granted 18 days, and those who have completed seven continuous years are granted 21 days. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Annual leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the yearly entitlement. Leave may be taken before it has fully accrued with the written approval of the department head.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Carried days expire on 31 March. Days beyond 5 are forfeited at year end and are not paid out, except where an employee was prevented from taking leave by a written instruction from management.

## Sick leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence of three or more consecutive working days. Sick leave does not accrue and cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 90 calendar days of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 10 working days. Parental leave must begin within six months of the birth or placement.

## Approval
Annual leave of 1 to 4 days is approved by the immediate manager. Leave of 5 or more consecutive days requires approval from the department head. Requests must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance, except for sick leave and bereavement leave.

Two members of the same team may not be on annual leave at the same time during a declared project freeze period.
