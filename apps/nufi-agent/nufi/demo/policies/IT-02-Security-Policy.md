# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
All accounts must use a password of at least 14 characters. Passwords must be changed every 180 days, and the previous 5 passwords may not be reused. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the finance system, and any account with administrative privileges.

Shared accounts are prohibited. Where a system cannot support individual accounts, the exception must be recorded in the security register and reviewed every 6 months.

## Devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and the endpoint agent installed. Personal devices may access company email only through the managed application, and may not store company files locally.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 4 hours of discovery, at any hour of the day.

## Remote access
Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN. Split tunnelling is disabled. Access from outside the country requires prior approval from the IT security lead, granted for a maximum of 30 days per request.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data may not be placed in any third-party service that has not completed a security review. Restricted data may not leave the country without written approval from the data protection officer.

## Incident reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the IT service desk immediately and no later than 1 hour after discovery. Staff must not attempt to investigate or remediate an incident themselves. The security team acknowledges reports within 2 hours and provides an initial assessment within 24 hours.
