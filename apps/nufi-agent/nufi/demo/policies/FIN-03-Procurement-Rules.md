# Procurement Rules

## Approval thresholds
Purchases up to 1,000,000 KRW may be approved by the team manager. Purchases from 1,000,001 to 10,000,000 KRW require department head approval. Purchases from 10,000,001 to 50,000,000 KRW require approval from the finance director. Anything above 50,000,000 KRW requires board approval.

Splitting a single purchase into smaller orders to stay under a threshold is prohibited and is treated as a disciplinary matter.

## Competitive quotes
Purchases above 5,000,000 KRW require at least three written quotes. Where three quotes cannot be obtained, a sole-source justification must be filed with the finance team before the order is placed.

## New vendors
A new vendor must complete the vendor registration form and pass a compliance check before the first order. The compliance check takes up to 10 working days. Vendors handling Confidential or Restricted data must additionally complete a security review.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days from invoice receipt. Terms shorter than 15 days require finance director approval. Prepayment is permitted only for software subscriptions and conference fees.

## Purchase orders
No work may begin before a purchase order has been issued. An invoice received without a matching purchase order is returned to the vendor unpaid.
