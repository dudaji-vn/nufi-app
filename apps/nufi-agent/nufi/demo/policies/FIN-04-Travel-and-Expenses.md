# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking
Domestic travel must be booked at least 7 days in advance, international travel at least 21 days in advance. Bookings made later require written justification from the department head.

## Class of travel
Domestic flights are economy class without exception. International flights under 6 hours are economy class. International flights of 6 hours or more may be premium economy. Business class requires approval from the finance director and is granted only for flights over 10 hours.

Rail travel is standard class for journeys under 3 hours and may be first class for longer journeys.

## Accommodation
The nightly accommodation limit is 150,000 KRW domestically and 250,000 KRW internationally. Amounts above the limit require prior approval and a written reason.

## Daily allowance
The daily allowance is 40,000 KRW for domestic travel and 80,000 KRW for international travel. The allowance covers meals and incidental costs. Meals paid for by the company or by a host are deducted from the allowance at one third of the daily rate per meal.

## Receipts and claims
Receipts are required for every expense above 20,000 KRW. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

## Non-reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, hotel minibar charges, and flight upgrades paid personally are not reimbursed.
